# Changelog

Release notes for every published Gloam build. Each entry matches a [GitHub Release](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases).

## [0.0.5](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/tag/v0.0.5) — 2026-09-14

### Added

- Set a scene on Desktop, Lock Screen, or Both, like on iPhone. Auto mode only changes the desktop.

### Changed

- Discover filters moods in place, and catalog cards set wallpaper the same way as your own scenes.
- New live scenes look as rich on the desktop as they do on the lock screen.
- Switching scenes fades on the desktop.
- Settings can turn off GPU desktop playback to save power.

### Fixed

- Live wallpapers loop without a visible cut.

### Download

[Gloam.dmg](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/download/v0.0.5/Gloam.dmg)

## [0.0.4](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/tag/v0.0.4) — 2026-09-08

### Added

- In-app updates: Gloam checks daily and installs signed updates for you.
- One editor for photo slideshows — add, remove, reorder, and retime photos.

### Changed

- Clicking a card sets the wallpaper, and downloads land in My Scenes immediately.

### Fixed

- Live wallpaper now follows every Mission Control desktop.

### Download

[Gloam.dmg](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/download/v0.0.4/Gloam.dmg)

## [0.0.3](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/tag/v0.0.3) — 2026-09-08

### Changed

- Navigation is now Desktop, Discover, and My Scenes.
- The menu bar is just what is playing, Pause, and Next.
- Settings is one compact scrollable page.

### Fixed

- Live wallpaper is back on the lock screen, and desktop video resumes after sleep.
- Search works on every page.

### Download

[Gloam.dmg](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/download/v0.0.3/Gloam.dmg)

## [0.0.2](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/tag/v0.0.2) — 2026-09-02

### Added

- Turn your own photos into a live slideshow.
- Assign scenes to moods, and create or delete moods of your own.

### Changed

- Slideshows render at your display’s native resolution, up to 5K.

### Fixed

- The scene you pick stays put — automatic switching no longer replaces it a minute later.

### Download

[Gloam.dmg](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/download/v0.0.2/Gloam.dmg)

## [0.0.1](https://github.com/gloam-app/gloamrelease/releases/tag/v0.0.1) — 2026-08-31

First feedback release for macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.

### Added

- Live video wallpapers on your desktop, with an optional lock screen.
- Import your own clips, or find new ones in Discover.
- No account, and your files never leave your Mac.
